# Assets 1.13.57 — Halloween 2026 Z2 map

The Halloween edition of Z2 (ROTK Halloween 2026 v185 from the designer), on top of the live assets 1.13.56. Temporary: assets 1.13.58 reverts it after Halloween.

On the map (`Z2.zone` of `Z2_x64_12`): string lights in orange / violet / ember, pumpkins, carved pumpkins and skulls in one yard in three, hay bales and field pumpkins, gravestones, body bags, coffins, open burning barrels, scarecrows, pumpkin farm signs readable from 400 m, and far string-light cells (one merged model per 40 m cell, 70 to 250 m). 1,547 trees near towns and in treelines become dead trees and stumps (`Z2_cell_0_*.cel` of `Z2_x64_0` and `Z2_x64_19`). No string lights on churches or over gas station double doors.

Lights and effects (`Z2Areas.xml`, effect tables, `.fxd`): street-light ground pools, fires in barrels and car cabins, bats, flickering lit ceiling panels, steady church candles; the map's lamp effects are lit at every hour. Store signs, lamp heads and big screens glow; the Showdown / Ranked Pro screens show the Halloween poster. The death loot bag (model 9581) looks like a pumpkin candy bag. Car boost and exhaust light flashes are off (the flame particles stay); police car night materials. Lit traffic lights lose the stock street-lamp light of their effect, which turned into a huge halo at night: `City_Props_StopLight` keeps its flashing signals without it (effect 5312 plays FX 1436, `StopLight_Flashing_All.fxd`), and `City_Props_StopLight_NoLeftTurnLight` flashes from 06:00 to 17:00 only (effect 5314), as there is no stock variant without the light.

Lighting (`Lighting_Z2_PS3.txt`): Day, Dusk and Dawn are exactly the live full daylight; only Twilight, Night and FalseDawn carry the orange Halloween night. Public lobbies keep their hour (10:00, 14:00 with the PS3 profile), so the night shows only where the hour is set to night.

Packaging:
- `Z2_x64_0`, `Z2_x64_12` and `Z2_x64_19` join the feed. The first commit ships them unchanged from the stock client, so the revert keeps them installed (with stock content) instead of uninstalling them.
- Every file the package placed in `assets_x64_2`, `_3`, `_4`, `_5`, `_6`, `_7`, `_8`, `_15` and `_16` goes to `assets_x64_0` instead: its copy wins over the other `assets_x64` packs (as for the Chinese taxi of 1.13.55), so players download two packs instead of eleven and `assets_x64_4/5/7` stay out of the feed.
- `data_x64_0` gets the 9,284 new models, textures and actors (under `rotk_halloween/`) and five replaced files. `ROTK_Halloween_Decorative_Military_Props_CoffinLiners_Single_RM.adr` is the designer's later copy with the coffin collision (`Military_Props_CoffinLiners_Single_RM.cdt`, stock in `assets_x64_4`).

Validated in game in the isolated two-client harness, on a live 1.13.56 client with the five rebuilt packs.

Assets only; the lobby hour setting is a separate server change.
